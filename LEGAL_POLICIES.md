## 3. Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Last Updated:** August 2026  
**Regulatory Compliance:** Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020

### 3.1 Order Cancellation Policy
1. **Cancellation by Customer (Pre-Dispatch):**
   - You may request order cancellation within **4 hours of placing the order** or before an Air Waybill (AWB) tracking number has been generated.
   - To cancel, email [ellacreationsindia@gmail.com](mailto:ellacreationsindia@gmail.com) or contact our WhatsApp concierge with your Order ID.
   - Upon confirmation, a 100% refund will be issued to your original payment method within 24–48 hours.
2. **Post-Dispatch Policy:**
   - Once an order has been packed, assigned a courier AWB, and handed over to the courier partner, **cancellations cannot be accepted**.
3. **Cancellation by Ella Creations:**
   - In rare events such as stock discrepancies, courier delivery blackouts, or failed fraud verification, we reserve the right to cancel your order with a prompt 100% refund.

### 3.2 Hygiene Standards & Final Sale Policy
Due to strict sanitary and health hygiene standards governing intimate body-contact jewelry (earrings, studs, chokers, nose jewelry, and bridal ornaments), **all items once delivered and opened are strictly non-returnable and non-exchangeable for change of mind or personal preference**.

### 3.3 Protection Against Transit Damage, Defects & Discrepancies
Every customer is 100% protected against transit damage or incorrect deliveries:
1. **Statutory 48-Hour Reporting Window:** You must report any physical damage, broken clasp, missing stone, or incorrect item within **48 hours of delivery**, as recorded by the courier timestamp.
2. **Mandatory Continuous Unboxing Video:**
   - A single, uncut, continuous video starting from the sealed outer tamper-evident courier bag, displaying the shipping label clearly, and capturing the unboxing and inspection of the piece is **mandatory**.
   - This requirement is essential to validate freight damage claims with insurance carriers.
3. **How to Submit a Claim:** Email the unboxing footage, photos, and your Order ID to [ellacreationsindia@gmail.com](mailto:ellacreationsindia@gmail.com).

### 3.4 Replacement & Refund Processing Time
1. **Quality Inspection:** Our quality desk inspects your claim within 24 to 48 hours.
2. **Resolution Options:**
   - **Complimentary Replacement:** A brand new piece of identical specification will be dispatched via priority express courier at no additional cost.
   - **Full Refund:** If the item is out of stock or retired, a 100% refund of the purchase price will be issued.
3. **Refund Timelines:**
   - **Prepaid Online Orders (Razorpay):** Credited back to the original source account (UPI / Card / Net Banking) within **5 to 7 business days**, subject to your bank’s processing cycle.
   - **Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders:** Credited via secure NEFT/IMPS bank transfer or UPI within 5 to 7 business days following receipt of your verified bank account details.

## 4. Shipping & Delivery Policy

**Last Updated:** August 2026  
**Operational Scope:** Pan-India Service Across 19,000+ PIN Codes

### 4.1 Order Processing & Dispatch Timelines
1. **Inspection & Packing:** Every piece undergoes artisanal inspection, velvet buffer wrapping, and sealing in a serialized tamper-evident courier pouch.
2. **Dispatch Window:**
   - **Standard Catalog Orders:** Dispatched within **24 to 48 business hours** following payment authorization (or phone verification for COD).
   - **Dispatch Operating Days:** Monday through Saturday (excluding national holidays and Sundays).
3. **Tracking Updates:** Real-time Air Waybill (AWB) tracking links are dispatched via SMS, WhatsApp, and registered email immediately upon logistics handover.

### 4.2 Domestic Delivery Procedures & Timeframes
We partner with premier courier networks via Shiprocket (including Bluedart, Delhivery, DTDC, and Smartr Logistics):
- **Tier 1 Metro Hubs (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Pune, Ahmedabad):** 2 to 4 business days.
- **Tier 2 & Tier 3 Cities / District Headquarters:** 4 to 6 business days.
- **Special Zones (North-Eastern States, J&K, Ladakh, Island Territories):** 5 to 8 business days.

### 4.3 Shipping Fees & Rates
- **Complimentary Free Shipping:** Standard insured shipping is completely free for all orders exceeding **₹999** across India.
- **Orders Below ₹999:** A nominal flat logistics fee of **₹99** applies.
- **Air Priority Express:** Optional expedited air transit routing available at checkout for **₹199**.

### 4.4 Tamper-Evident Packaging & Delivery Verification Protocol
- All shipments are dispatched in branded, high-strength, **tear-resistant tamper-evident polybags** with sequential serial coding.
- **Delivery Inspection:** Customers must examine the outer courier bag before accepting the shipment. If the package appears torn, cut, retaped with plain tape, or tampered with, **please refuse delivery immediately** and report it to our concierge.
- **OTP Verification:** For high-value jewelry orders, delivery personnel will verify physical handover via a dynamic One-Time Password (OTP) sent to your registered mobile phone.

### 4.5 Bulk Shipping & Bridal Troupe Orders
- **Applicability:** Orders comprising 5 or more pieces, customized bridesmaid gift sets, or orders exceeding ₹25,000.
- **Production & Dispatch Lead Time:** Requires **7 to 14 business days** to allow custom handcrafted setting, multi-stage electroplate curing, and personalized gift tags.
- **Dedicated Logistics Coordinator:** A dedicated concierge manager is assigned to coordinate staggered wedding event delivery dates.
- **Special Declared Transit Insurance:** Handled under dedicated declared-value commercial transit insurance coverage.

### 4.6 Failed Deliveries & Return to Origin (RTO)
- Courier partners make up to **three (3) delivery attempts** accompanied by phone and SMS alerts.
- In cases of incorrect address, customer unavailability, or unjustified refusal, the parcel is returned to our fulfillment hub (RTO).
- A standard re-shipping fee of ₹150 applies for re-dispatching RTO parcels.
